How we build

Fewer apps, made properly

One job each

Every app does a single thing and gets out of the way. If a feature doesn't earn its place, it doesn't ship.

Respect the user

No ads mid-task, no dark patterns, no selling data. The experience belongs to the person using it.

Built to last

Shared, battle-tested foundations across every app, so fixes and improvements reach all of them.

Accessible & fast

Works on modest phones, in many languages, in light and dark — because most of the world uses apps that way.
