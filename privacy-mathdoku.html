Privacy Policy for Mathdoku

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

IDL Dynamics ("We", "Our", "Company") built the Mathdoku app as an ad-supported game. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page informs players about our policies regarding the collection and use of information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Mathdoku does not require you to provide sensitive personal information (like your real name, address, or precise GPS location) to play. We do, however, process some data that is linked to your device: a random device identifier, device type, operating system version, and gameplay statistics, used to operate the game and improve the user experience.

To keep your progress and show usage statistics, the app stores on our servers a random device identifier created by the app, your game progress and leaderboard scores, the app version, the platform (iOS or Android), language, time zone and, on Android only, the install source campaign label (for example, which campaign the app was installed from). This data is not linked to your name or email address. If you allow notifications, a push token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging is stored so that we can send game reminders; you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We use third-party services, specifically Google AdMob, to display advertisements within the app, and analytics services (such as Google Firebase Analytics on Android) to understand usage. These providers may collect identifiers (such as Advertising IDs or device identifiers) and usage data to provide relevant ads and statistics. In the EEA and the UK an ad consent window is shown; in other regions, ads may be personalized according to your device's ad settings and, on iOS, the tracking permission; you can turn this off in your device settings. We strictly comply with the app-ads.txt authorization protocols to ensure ad safety.

Optional in-app purchases (such as removing ads) are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase status; we never receive your payment card details.

iOS tracking. On iOS we ask for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before using the advertising identifier for personalised ads.

Data controller, nickname, retention and deletion

Data controller and rights. IDL Dynamics is the data controller. We process the data above to run the game, keep your progress, show leaderboards, send optional reminders, show ads and measure usage (legal bases: performance of the service, legitimate interest, and consent where required). To exercise your rights (access, correction, deletion, objection; under KVKK Art. 11 and GDPR) write to idldynamics@gmail.com with your in-app player ID.

Leaderboard nickname. The nickname you choose is shown publicly on the global leaderboard. Do not use your real name.

Retention and deletion. Device-linked profile data is kept while the app is in use and deleted on request: email us at idldynamics@gmail.com with your player ID.

3. International Transfers

Our hosting, notification, payment, analytics and advertising service providers may be located outside your country (including outside Türkiye); your data is transferred to them only as needed to provide these services.
