Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All data (student info, messages, attendance logs) is stored in highly secure cloud servers encrypted with Row-Level Security (RLS). No data is shared with third parties.

How does location-verified attendance work?

When a session starts, the app compares the student's GPS location with the classroom coordinates. Only students within the designated area are marked as present, preventing remote or proxy sign-ins.

Do I need to share my phone number to message students?

No. Our built-in messaging module allows you to communicate solely through your app profile. Your personal phone number and social media remain completely private.

Can I export the attendance data?

Yes. You can generate weekly, monthly, or term-based reports and download them as Excel or PDF files to share with administration or parents.
