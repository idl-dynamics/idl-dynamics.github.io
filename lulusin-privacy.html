Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

1Information We Collect

Lulusin collects the minimum data necessary to function as a self-study exam-preparation tool for Universitas Terbuka (UT) students: account information (email address, and optionally a display name shown on leaderboards), your selected courses and UAS exam date, your study history and answers (used to power the spaced-repetition schedule and readiness statistics), and a device identifier used solely to deliver push notifications.

2Authentication

You may sign in with email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple. We do not store your Google or Apple account password. Email/password credentials are managed securely by our authentication provider, Supabase Auth.

3Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store via RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information — this is handled entirely by Google, Apple, and RevenueCat according to their own privacy and security standards.

4Data Location and Cross-Border Transfer

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in Singapore (AWS region ap-southeast-1). Any cross-border transfer of your personal data is carried out with equivalent protections, in accordance with Indonesia's Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP).

5What We Don't Collect

Lulusin does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or advertising identifier. We do not use any third-party advertising network, and the app does not display ads.

6Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase — hosting, authentication, and data storage
  • OneSignal — push notifications
  • RevenueCat — subscription purchase processing
  • Google / Apple — optional sign-in

These services handle your data according to their own security and privacy standards.

7Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. Your information is never sold to third parties.

8Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time by:

  • Using the "Delete Account" option within the application's Profile settings.
  • Sending an email to idldynamics@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request - Lulusin".

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your account information and associated data from our servers within 30 days.

9Disclaimer

Lulusin is an independent self-study tool. Questions are composed based on official Universitas Terbuka course materials (BMP), not actual exam papers, and are intended to help you study the material rather than predict exam content. Lulusin is not officially affiliated with Universitas Terbuka.

10Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at: idldynamics@gmail.com
