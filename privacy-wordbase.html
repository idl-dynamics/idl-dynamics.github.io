Privacy Policy for WordBase

Last Updated: July 7, 2026

IDL Dynamics ("We", "Our", "Company") respects the privacy of the users ("You", "Player") of the WordBase application. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account Data

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture to create and identify your account.

Gameplay Data

Game history, moves, scores, statistics, league standings, and in-app chat messages you send to other players or bots.

Virtual Currency & Purchases

Your Coin balance, transaction history, and in-app purchase records. Actual payment processing is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat; we do not receive or store your payment card details.

Device & Usage Data

Device type, operating system version, app version, crash and error logs, and advertising identifiers (for ad-supported features).

Notification Data

A push notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging and OneSignal) so we can deliver turn reminders, gift alerts, and other in-app notifications you have enabled.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to operate core gameplay (matchmaking, scoring, leagues), maintain your Coin balance and purchase history, send the notifications you've opted into, respond to support requests, detect cheating and abuse, and improve app stability.

3. Virtual Currency ("Coins")

Coins are a virtual in-app unit used within WordBase. Coins have no real-world monetary or material value, cannot be exchanged for cash under any circumstances, and are not valid or usable outside of WordBase. Coins cannot be transferred directly between users, sold, exchanged, or otherwise assigned to any third party.

WordBase is a word/trivia skill game. It is not offered, and must not be interpreted, as real-money gambling, an investment vehicle, or a means of earning income. Purchasing Coins is entirely optional — you may continue playing for free or earn Coins by watching ads. This feature is not designed for users under the age of 13.

When you purchase Coins, they are credited to your account once payment is confirmed by the applicable store (App Store or Google Play). Refund requests are subject solely to the applicable store's refund policy; we do not issue direct cash refunds.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — authentication and database storage
  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications, crash reporting, and analytics
  • OneSignal — push notification delivery
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase and subscription management
  • Google AdMob — rewarded and interstitial advertising
  • Google / Apple Sign-In — authentication providers

5. Data Sharing & Security

We never sell your personal data to third parties. Data is protected using Row-Level Security (RLS) and is only accessible by systems and personnel necessary to operate the service.

6. Children's Privacy

WordBase is not directed at children under 13, and purchasing or spending Coins is not designed for users under that age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Deletion

You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the in-app "Delete Account" option in Settings, or by contacting us below. We will delete your account information within 30 days of a verified request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
