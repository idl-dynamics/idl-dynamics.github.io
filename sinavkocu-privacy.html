Sınav Koçu

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 22, 2026

1Information We Collect

Sınav Koçu collects minimal data necessary to function as an exam-preparation tool: basic account information (email, name) via Google or Apple Sign-In, your selected university/department/class year, study progress and answer history, and daily streak/statistics.

2Authentication

We use Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple exclusively for authentication. We do not collect or store passwords. Your name and email are provided to us by Google/Apple with your consent during sign-in.

3Purchases

Course package purchases are processed through the Apple App Store / Google Play via RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information — this is handled entirely by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat according to their own privacy and security standards.

4Community-Shared Content

If you upload exam papers via the "Upload Exam Paper" feature, the file (and any questions extracted from it) may be shared anonymously with the wider student community using the app, to help build a shared question bank. We do not attach your name or identity to uploaded content visible to other users.

5Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase — authentication and data storage
  • Cloudflare R2 — offline question-file storage
  • OneSignal — push notifications
  • RevenueCat — purchase processing

These services handle your data according to their own security and privacy standards.

6Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data. Your information is never sold to third parties.

7Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time by:

  • Using the "Delete Account" option within the application settings.
  • Sending an email to idldynamics@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your account information and associated data from our servers within 30 days.

8Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at: idldynamics@gmail.com
