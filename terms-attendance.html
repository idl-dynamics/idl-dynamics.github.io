1. Description of Service

IDL Attendance is a classroom attendance tool. Instructors create classes and open attendance sessions; students join those sessions and record their attendance. Depending on how the instructor configures a session, attendance may be recorded by scanning a QR code, by digital confirmation, or manually by the instructor. Where the instructor sets a distance requirement, the App checks your location at the moment of check-in to confirm you are near the classroom.
